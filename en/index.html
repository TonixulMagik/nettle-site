Support tickets Available

Members open a private channel with your team from a panel menu. Your team claims the ticket, can add or remove people, hand it over to a colleague and close it, and the conversation is posted as a transcript in the channel you choose.

ExampleA member picks "Report a player" on the panel, answers up to three short questions, and a private channel opens with the team of that category.

Commands

  • /ticket setup
  • /ticket add
  • /ticket remove
  • /ticket transfer
  • /ticket close
  • /ticket blacklist
  • +1

Good to know

Team members need one of the category's roles; only the person who claimed a ticket (or an admin) can close it or manage its members.

Role panels, giveaways, polls and birthdays Available

The everyday tools of a community server in one bot. Panels where members pick their own roles, giveaways with entry requirements, polls with menu voting, and birthday announcements.

ExamplePost a panel with your notification roles as buttons, start a 12 hour giveaway for members who joined at least 7 days ago, and let Nettle wish happy birthday around 10 AM Romania time.

Commands

  • /panou roluri
  • /giveaway start
  • /giveaway end
  • /giveaway cancel
  • /giveaway lista
  • /sondaj
  • +1

Good to know

Nettle's highest role must sit above every role it hands out, and the person building a panel must also be above those roles (the owner is exempt).

Moderation and the spam trap Available

Ban (also by ID), kick, timeout, slow mode and bulk delete with clear confirmations and the reason written to Discord's audit log. The honeypot is a trap channel. Spam accounts that post in it are caught automatically.

ExampleCreate an empty channel nobody has a reason to write in, run /honeypot seteaza on it with the Ban action, and exempt your staff role with /honeypot scutire.

Commands

  • /ban
  • /unban
  • /kick
  • /timeout
  • /untimeout
  • /purge
  • +5

Good to know

Permissions come from Discord only.

Temporary voice channels Available

Members join one "door" channel and get their own voice channel, which disappears when everyone leaves. The owner of the channel controls it from a panel. Name, user limit, status, lock, unlock, allow, block and invite.

ExampleSomeone joins the door channel and is moved into a new channel named after them; when the last person leaves, the channel is deleted.

Commands

  • /vocale

Good to know

Up to 25 temporary channels at a time per server, and anti-spam limits on creating, renaming and inviting.

More about Voice

Media conversion, emoji and stickers Available

Convert pictures, PDFs, audio and video between common formats without leaving Discord, grab any custom emoji as an image, and turn a picture into a server sticker in one step.

Example/convert with a PNG attached, then choose JPG or PDF from the panel; /sticker add with a picture, a name and a matching emoji.

Commands

  • /convert
  • /emoji
  • /sticker add

Good to know

Files up to 25 MB, results up to 9 MB, audio and video up to 6 minutes, documents up to 20 pages; the type is checked from the file's content, not its name.

Everyday tools and setup Available

Small tools members actually use. A calculator, time in any city, Discord timestamps, colour codes, QR codes, reminders, server, role and channel info, a message builder for announcements, and two right-click actions on any message (save it to your DMs, or turn it into a quote picture). /setari shows the owner what Nettle can do on the server and what permissions it is missing.

Example/amintire pune cand:în 2 ore text:Scoate prăjitura din cuptor, and two hours later Nettle pings you in the same channel.

Commands

  • /help
  • /about
  • /setari
  • /serverprofile avatar set
  • /serverprofile avatar reset
  • /serverprofile banner set
  • +15

Good to know

Reminders. From 1 minute to 30 days ahead, at most 300 characters, at most 10 pending per person; they are delivered in the channel where they were set, with a ping only for you, never by DM.

Build your world

Play, grow, and know the rules.

Small games and a score that stays on your server. The rules are written right next to each feature.

  • Games and fun

    Nine games right inside Discord. Slots, blackjack, mines, street dice, card war, hangman, snake, rock paper scissors and tic-tac-toe, several of them playable against Nettle or as a duel with a friend. Plus quick fun commands like 8ball, ship, dice, coin flip and friendly roleplay actions.

  • Chips, a per-server game score

    Chips give games something to play for without any real money. Everyone starts with 1,000 chips on each server, gets a daily top-up of 300, can work once a day with /munca, and climbs the server's chips leaderboard.

  • Profiles and activity

    A profile card drawn as an image, with picture, banner, badges and roles, plus quick lookups of anyone's avatar, banner and public account information, and the server's activity top for messages and voice time.

Good to know

  • Nettle is free. Nothing in it can be bought with real money, and nothing in it can be exchanged for real money.
  • Games Games with a bet use chips, which have no real value (see Chips). Snake, rock paper scissors and tic-tac-toe use no chips. Hangman is free to enter and can award a limited number of chip prizes per day. At least 3 seconds between two games started by the same person. /roleplay works without animated GIFs on public servers for now.
  • Chips Chips have no real-world value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for anything, and there is no admin command that hands out chips. Each server has its own chips; nothing carries over between servers.
  • Profiles Profiles show only information Discord makes public. For /activitate, Nettle counts messages (at most one every 15 seconds) and minutes in voice, on each server. It does not read or keep what you write.
