Try it

Try Nettle right here

A pretend Discord, in your browser. Type / and pick a command, or press the / button for the list. Nettle answers like on a real server, in a short version.

Heads up. Nettle itself speaks Romanian. Every command, button and message the bot sends is in Romanian.

Opening in steps. Before the launch, only /about (anywhere) and /serverprofile (Administrator only) are live on servers. At launch, the full command list is published to every server automatically. Launch day is 1 November 2026 at 8 PM Romania time.

Interactive demo. Nothing is sent to Discord.
