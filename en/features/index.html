Support tickets

Members open a private channel with your team from a panel menu. Your team claims the ticket, can add or remove people, hand it over to a colleague and close it, and the conversation is posted as a transcript in the channel you choose.

Example
A member picks "Report a player" on the panel, answers up to three short questions, and a private channel opens with the team of that category.
How to set it up
The server owner or anyone with Manage Server runs /ticket setup (also reachable from /setari). Choose the panel channel, the Discord category for ticket channels, the transcript channel, then up to 10 ticket categories, each with up to 5 team roles, up to 3 opening questions and an optional ping of the team roles.
Good to know
Team members need one of the category's roles; only the person who claimed a ticket (or an admin) can close it or manage its members. Nettle needs View Channel, Send Messages, Read Message History and Manage Channels in the ticket category, or tickets cannot be opened. To ping a team role, that role must be set as mentionable in Discord, because Nettle does not ask for the Mention @everyone permission. A person can have 1 to 3 open tickets at a time (set by the admin). Discord itself allows at most 50 channels in a category. Transcripts cover up to 500 messages and a limited number of attachments.
Commands
  • /ticket setup
  • /ticket add
  • /ticket remove
  • /ticket transfer
  • /ticket close
  • /ticket blacklist
  • /ticket unblacklist

Role panels, giveaways, polls and birthdays

The everyday tools of a community server in one bot. Panels where members pick their own roles, giveaways with entry requirements, polls with menu voting, and birthday announcements.

Example
Post a panel with your notification roles as buttons, start a 12 hour giveaway for members who joined at least 7 days ago, and let Nettle wish happy birthday around 10 AM Romania time.
How to set it up
Role panels. Anyone with Manage Roles runs /panou roluri and builds the panel in an editor (menu or buttons, one role or several). Giveaways. Anyone with Manage Server, or people and roles the owner allows in /setari. Polls. Anyone with Manage Messages. Birthdays. Members set their own date with /ziua; in the same panel, people with Manage Server pick the announcement channel and switch announcements on.
Good to know
Nettle's highest role must sit above every role it hands out, and the person building a panel must also be above those roles (the owner is exempt). Panels refuse roles with dangerous permissions (for example Administrator, Manage Server, Ban Members), managed roles and @everyone. Up to 25 roles per panel and 10 panels per server by default. Up to 10 active giveaways per server, lasting at most 60 days. Polls have up to 9 options. Birthdays store day and month only, never the year, per server; the date can be changed once every 30 days, no rewards are given and nothing is sent by DM.
Commands
  • /panou roluri
  • /giveaway start
  • /giveaway end
  • /giveaway cancel
  • /giveaway lista
  • /sondaj
  • /ziua

Temporary voice channels

Members join one "door" channel and get their own voice channel, which disappears when everyone leaves. The owner of the channel controls it from a panel. Name, user limit, status, lock, unlock, allow, block and invite.

Example
Someone joins the door channel and is moved into a new channel named after them; when the last person leaves, the channel is deleted.
How to set it up
Anyone with Manage Server runs /vocale and picks the category where the door and the members' channels appear.
Good to know
Up to 25 temporary channels at a time per server, and anti-spam limits on creating, renaming and inviting. Nettle only ever deletes channels it created itself. It needs Manage Channels and Move Members in that category.
Commands
  • /vocale

Games and fun

Nine games right inside Discord. Slots, blackjack, mines, street dice, card war, hangman, snake, rock paper scissors and tic-tac-toe, several of them playable against Nettle or as a duel with a friend. Plus quick fun commands like 8ball, ship, dice, coin flip and friendly roleplay actions.

Example
Challenge a friend to /barbut with 100 chips each, or try to beat Nettle at /xo on the hard level.
How to set it up
Nothing to set up. Games are on by default. On the Jocuri (Games) page you can switch each game on or off, choose whether it is played in the open or privately (only the player sees it), set the minimum and maximum bet and the pause between games, and start a new season. This page is open to the server owner, the people the owner names in /setari, anyone with Manage Server, and the roles or people given access to games in /setari. It opens from /setari or from the „Setări jocuri” (Game settings) button under „Fisele mele” (My chips), on a game's panel.
Good to know
Games with a bet use chips, which have no real value (see Chips). Snake, rock paper scissors and tic-tac-toe use no chips. Hangman is free to enter and can award a limited number of chip prizes per day. At least 3 seconds between two games started by the same person. /roleplay works without animated GIFs on public servers for now.

Chips, a per-server game score

Chips give games something to play for without any real money. Everyone starts with 1,000 chips on each server, gets a daily top-up of 300, can work once a day with /munca, and climbs the server's chips leaderboard.

Example
Run /munca for a shift paid between 100 and 200 chips, then check your balance and your place in the top with /banca.
How to set it up
Nothing to set up. A new season resets the chips leaderboard on that server. It is started from the Jocuri (Games) page by the same people who set up the games, that is the server owner, the people the owner names in /setari, anyone with Manage Server, and the roles or people given access to games in /setari.
Good to know
Chips have no real-world value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for anything, and there is no admin command that hands out chips. Each server has its own chips; nothing carries over between servers.
Commands
  • /munca
  • /banca

Profiles and activity

A profile card drawn as an image, with picture, banner, badges and roles, plus quick lookups of anyone's avatar, banner and public account information, and the server's activity top for messages and voice time.

Example
/profil cine:@Andrei shows Andrei's card; /avatar gives the picture in several sizes.
How to set it up
Nothing to set up.
Good to know
Profiles show only information Discord makes public. For /activitate, Nettle counts messages (at most one every 15 seconds) and minutes in voice, on each server. It does not read or keep what you write.
Commands
  • /profil
  • /avatar
  • /banner
  • /userinfo
  • /activitate

Moderation and the spam trap

Ban (also by ID), kick, timeout, slow mode and bulk delete with clear confirmations and the reason written to Discord's audit log. The honeypot is a trap channel. Spam accounts that post in it are caught automatically.

Example
Create an empty channel nobody has a reason to write in, run /honeypot seteaza on it with the Ban action, and exempt your staff role with /honeypot scutire.
How to set it up
Each command is shown by default only to people with the matching Discord permission (Ban Members, Kick Members, Timeout Members, Manage Messages, Manage Channels). The honeypot needs Ban Members and Manage Server. Admins can change who sees a command in Server Settings, Integrations.
Good to know
Permissions come from Discord only. Nettle's role must sit above the members it moderates, and the moderator must also be above the target; keeping Nettle's role below your staff and admin roles is recommended. No temporary bans and no DM to the sanctioned person. /purge deletes up to 100 messages, only ones newer than 14 days, and skips pinned messages. Timeouts last at most 28 days; slow mode at most 6 hours. The honeypot actions are alert only, softban, ban or a 24 hour timeout; it does not read what the message says.
Commands
  • /ban
  • /unban
  • /kick
  • /timeout
  • /untimeout
  • /purge
  • /slowmode
  • /honeypot seteaza
  • /honeypot scutire
  • /honeypot stare
  • /honeypot opreste

Media conversion, emoji and stickers

Convert pictures, PDFs, audio and video between common formats without leaving Discord, grab any custom emoji as an image, and turn a picture into a server sticker in one step.

Example
/convert with a PNG attached, then choose JPG or PDF from the panel; /sticker add with a picture, a name and a matching emoji.
How to set it up
Nothing to set up for /convert. Adding emoji or stickers to the server needs the Create Expressions permission, for the person and for Nettle.
Good to know
Files up to 25 MB, results up to 9 MB, audio and video up to 6 minutes, documents up to 20 pages; the type is checked from the file's content, not its name. Temporary files are deleted when the job ends. Stickers. A still picture up to 2 MB.
Commands
  • /convert
  • /emoji
  • /sticker add

Everyday tools and setup

Small tools members actually use. A calculator, time in any city, Discord timestamps, colour codes, QR codes, reminders, server, role and channel info, a message builder for announcements, and two right-click actions on any message (save it to your DMs, or turn it into a quote picture). /setari shows the owner what Nettle can do on the server and what permissions it is missing.

Example
/amintire pune cand:în 2 ore text:Scoate prăjitura din cuptor, and two hours later Nettle pings you in the same channel.
How to set it up
Most tools need no setup. /setari is for the server owner and the people the owner names. /serverprofile (Administrator only) gives Nettle its own picture and banner on your server. /embed needs Manage Messages.
Good to know
Reminders. From 1 minute to 30 days ahead, at most 300 characters, at most 10 pending per person; they are delivered in the channel where they were set, with a ping only for you, never by DM. Times are read in Romania time unless you choose a time zone. Right-click actions work in the client under Apps.
