Story

Our story so far

Nettle is small on purpose. A small team, one raccoon, and a lot of care.

01

Made in Romania, by a small team.

Nettle is made by a small team in Romania. It is not a company. When you write to Nettle's support, you talk to the people who make it.

02

Free. Really free.

Nettle costs nothing. Game chips and coins are not money. You cannot buy them, sell them or trade them for anything real.

Nothing in Nettle is for sale.

03

Careful with your data.

Nettle keeps as little as it can. The Privacy Policy says what it keeps, why it keeps it, and how to have it deleted. It is written in plain words.

Read the Privacy Policy

04

Romanian first, open to all.

Nettle itself speaks Romanian. Every command, button and message the bot sends is in Romanian.

This website is in English and Romanian, so anyone can see what Nettle does before adding it.

05

Still growing.

Nettle is still being built. Commands get added, renamed and sometimes removed. Sometimes things break. Big changes are shared on the support server when possible.

06

And the raccoon?

The raccoon is Nettle's guide. It is curious and a little nosy, and it loves showing you things. Look around. It hides on this site.
